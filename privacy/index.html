Privacy Policy — Just Kill All The Zombies

Last updated: 11 October 2026

English · Español

Just Kill All The Zombies ("the game") is developed and published by El Barba Games ("we"). This policy describes what the Android version of the game collects, why, and who receives it. The web and desktop versions of the game collect nothing and contain no advertising.

What the game itself collects

Nothing. The game has no account of its own, no analytics of its own, no crash reporting of its own, and no server. It does not ask for any Android permission at runtime, and it stores nothing on your device: super powers earned by watching ads last only for the stage they were earned in. The two Google services below are the only things that receive anything.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android version shows rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Ads are only ever shown when you choose to watch one (to continue after dying, or to bank a super power); there are no interstitial or banner ads.

To serve and measure those ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • Advertising ID (the resettable Android advertising identifier) and the App Set ID;
  • IP address, which Google uses to estimate your approximate location;
  • Ad interactions (that an ad was requested, shown, completed, clicked or closed);
  • Diagnostic information about the SDK's own performance.

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. It is sent over encrypted connections. We do not receive any of it in identifiable form; we only see aggregate reports in the AdMob console. How Google handles it is described in Google's privacy policy, and in Google's description of the data the Ads SDK collects.

Test builds of the game distributed to testers show Google's test ads, which carry a "Test Ad" label; the same data is collected for them.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

The Android version keeps two score tables through Google Play Games, one for each difficulty. The Play Games SDK signs you in with your Google account when the game starts; that is the only sign-in the game has, and the game never sees your password. It does not use Play Games achievements or cloud saves.

What is sent to Google, and when:

  • Your Play Games profile — the gamer name and avatar you chose for Play Games;
  • A score, each time you clear a stage. It is built from how far you got, how many zombies you killed and how long you took, and says nothing about you.

Your gamer name and your best score are visible on the leaderboard to other players of the game. Whether you appear there is controlled by your Play Games profile visibility, not by us: a profile set to private still records your scores but does not show them to anyone else.

How Google handles this is described in Google's privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Personalised advertising and consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any ad is loaded, through Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time from Privacy options on the game's main menu, which appears whenever that choice applies to you.
  • Leaderboards. Set your Play Games profile to private, or sign out of Play Games, from the Google Play Games app. The game plays exactly the same either way.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Deleting it means the SDK receives a string of zeros instead of an identifier.
  • Opt out of personalised ads system-wide from the same Android settings screen.

Children

The game is not directed at children. Its content rating reflects its violence, and it is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is held by Google under Google's retention policies; use the advertising ID controls above to reset or delete the identifier that data is keyed on. Your scores are held by Google Play Games against your Play Games profile; they are deleted when you delete that profile's data from the Google Play Games app.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting anything else, this policy will be updated and the "last updated" date changed before that version is released.
